> One Pager Tailoring

Turns your real experience into a One Pager built for a specific role, in the words that role uses. It selects, translates and reframes what you have actually done. It never invents a line.

1 How the engine works
01Pools your experience. One CV or several (old One Pagers, LinkedIn export) read as a single source, deduplicated.
02Enriches what's thin. A short, skippable round of questions surfaces missing metrics and scope.
03Translates the gaps. When the role asks for something your CV doesn't show, it proposes a bridge and asks, so real experience surfaces instead of being dropped.
04Selects and reframes. Picks the 3 to 7 strongest experiences and aligns their wording to the role where the work genuinely matches.
05Builds the deck. Marks the matching phrases in purple, generates the .pptx, and explains every choice in a change note.
2 What to bring, each time
Your experience documents
One CV, or several. More angles covered means more to draw from.
A headshot photo
Square crop, about 1.22 inches, for the photo frame.
The job description
A link, or the text pasted in. This is what everything is tailored to.
The agent asks for anything missing before it starts, and it replies in your language, whichever one you write in.
3 Set it up once, then only bring the job description
  1. In Claude.ai, enable Code execution and file creation under Settings, Capabilities. Without it the skill cannot build files.
  2. Go to Customize, Skills, click + Create skill, then Upload a skill, and select the AC_OP_tailoring .zip.
  3. Turn the skill on with its toggle.
  1. Create a Project (name it "One Pager") and upload your CVs and photo to Project Knowledge. They stay loaded for good.
  2. Open a chat inside the Project and say: "Tailor my One Pager for this role", with the job description.
  3. Get the .pptx plus its change note. When your experience changes, replace the file in Project Knowledge.
4 Get the most out of it
🎤
Answer by voice, not by typing
For experiences and results, dictate your answers. Spoken, you give more detail and nuance than polished text. Save typing for short lists like tools or dates.
📚
Load every CV you have
Spread across an operations CV and a strategy one? Load both. The agent merges them and asks which is primary if it needs to.
🔗
Take the bridge questions seriously
When it asks "you did X, isn't that what the role calls Y?", that is your chance to claim real experience you would otherwise leave off the page. Be specific, and honest.
✅
Trust the gap flags
If the role wants something you have not done, it says so rather than faking it. A One Pager that survives scrutiny is the point.
One honest rule, everywhere. Nothing reaches the page unless your documents support it or you confirm it in the chat. The skill aligns terminology only where the underlying work is truly the same. It will not invent skills, titles, metrics or scope.
